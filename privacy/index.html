Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026.

This policy explains how Pathway Formation, a product of Pathway Devs, a department of John Gault & Sons ("we", "us"), collects and uses information on pathwaydevs.software. Operator and contact: John Gault & Sons, 3933 S Lotz Rd, Suite 219, Canton, MI 48188, support@pathwaydevs.org.

Information we collect

  • Order information you enter: company name, purpose and management type; principal office, mailing and registered agent addresses; member and organizer names, titles and ownership percentages; your name and email address; add-ons you select.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by Stripe on Stripe's own page. Card details go to Stripe, not to us. We receive payment confirmation details such as amount, date and the order reference.
  • Browser storage: the site stores your wizard progress and a random customer ID in your browser's local storage so your draft and orders stay available on that device.
  • Analytics (only with your consent): if you click Accept on the cookie notice, Google Analytics 4 collects usage data such as pages viewed, device and approximate location. If you decline or make no choice, the analytics script is not loaded.
  • Messages: anything you send to support@pathwaydevs.org.

How we use it

  • To prepare, review and file your formation documents and any add-ons you order.
  • To send order confirmations and service messages, and to answer questions.
  • To record payments and keep business and tax records.
  • To understand site usage and improve the site (analytics, with consent).

Public records

Documents filed with a state agency, such as articles of organization, become public records under that state's law. Information you put on a filing (for example the company name, addresses, registered agent and, in some states, organizer or member names) will be available to the public through the state.

Service providers we share with

  • State filing agencies, to file the documents you order.
  • Stripe (payments) — stripe.com/privacy.
  • Netlify (website hosting and order storage) — netlify.com/privacy.
  • Our email provider, to deliver order emails.
  • Google (Analytics, only with consent) — policies.google.com/privacy.

We may also disclose information when required by law, such as in response to a valid subpoena or court order.

Retention

We keep order and payment records for as long as needed to provide the service and to meet tax and recordkeeping requirements, including the IRS guidance that records supporting income and expenses be kept for the applicable period of limitations (IRS: How long should I keep records?).

Your choices

  • You can decline analytics on the cookie notice, or clear your browser's local storage to reset your choice.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information by emailing support@pathwaydevs.org. Records we must keep by law, and information already filed with a state, stay as required.
  • California residents: this policy is posted as required by the California Online Privacy Protection Act (Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code §§ 22575–22579). Our site takes no special action in response to browser "Do Not Track" signals; the analytics consent choice above controls tracking.

Children

The service is for people forming a business and is intended for adults. Under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (15 U.S.C. § 6501 et seq.), contact us if you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information and we will delete it.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS and payments are handled by Stripe. Order records are stored with our hosting provider and staff access requires a key.
